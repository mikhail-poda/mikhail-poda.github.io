# Topic: Broken, Flawed, Damaged

| **Hebrew**    | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                         | **Notes / Example Usage**                                                        |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **שָׁבוּר**      | *shavúr*          | broken                              | Physically broken; also emotionally devastated. **כיסא שבור** – a broken chair. |
| **מְקוּלְקָל**    | *mekulkál*        | broken, out of order; spoiled       | Common for machines, devices, food, or something not functioning properly. **המזגן מקולקל** – the air conditioner is broken. |
| **פָּגוּם**      | *pagúm*           | defective, flawed, damaged          | Has an inherent defect or reduced quality. **מוצר פגום** – a defective product. |
| **לָקוּי**      | *lakúi*           | deficient, impaired, faulty         | Formal; something has a weakness or does not meet the required standard. **תכנון לקוי** – flawed planning. |
| **נִזּוֹק**      | *nizók*           | damaged                             | Formal; has sustained damage. **מבנה ניזוק** – a damaged building. |

## Special Cases

| **Hebrew**   | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                         | **Notes / Example Usage**                                                        |
| ------------ | ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **פָּגוּעַ**     | *pagúa*           | damaged, injured, harmed            | Used for people, systems, reputation, or objects harmed by an event. **מערכת פגועה** – a damaged system. |
| **סָדוּק**     | *sadúk*           | cracked                             | Has a crack but is not necessarily completely broken. **מסך סדוק** – a cracked screen. |
| **חָבוּל**     | *chavúl*          | bruised, battered                   | Physically injured or visibly battered. **פנים חבולות** – a bruised face. |
| **הָרוּס**     | *harús*           | ruined, wrecked, destroyed          | More severe than merely broken; also emotionally exhausted. **בית הרוס** – a ruined house. |
| **קָרוּעַ**     | *karúa*           | torn, ripped                        | Used for fabric, paper, cables, or figuratively for inner conflict. **חולצה קרועה** – a torn shirt. |
| **מְעוּוָת**    | *me'uvát*         | distorted, deformed                 | Its shape, meaning, or structure has been altered incorrectly. **תמונה מעוותת** – a distorted image. |
| **דָּפוּק**     | *dafúk*           | messed up, broken, defective        | Very colloquial and sometimes insulting. **המחשב הזה דפוק** – this computer is messed up. |
