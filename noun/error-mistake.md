# Topic: Error, Mistake, Inaccuracy

| **Hebrew**     | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                      | **Example**                                                |
| -------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **טָעוּת**      | *ta'út*           | mistake, error                   | **טָעוּת אֱנוֹשׁ** – human error.                          |
| **שְׁגִיאָה**     | *shgi'á*          | error, often formal or technical | **שגיאת כתיב** – a spelling error.                         |
| **אִי־דִּיּוּק**   | *i-diyúk*         | inaccuracy, imprecision          | **יש כאן אי־דיוק קטן** – there is a small inaccuracy here. |
