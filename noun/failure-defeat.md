# Topic: Failure, Defeat

## Failure

| **Hebrew**   | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                             | **Example**                                                  |
| ------------ | ----------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **כִּישָּׁלוֹן**   | *kishalón*        | failure, lack of success                | **הניסוי הסתיים בכישלון** – the experiment ended in failure. |
| **כֶּשֶׁל**      | *késhel*          | failure, functional or systemic failure | **כשל מערכתי** – a systemic failure.                         |

## Loss, Defeat

| **Hebrew**   | **Transcription** | **Meaning**  | **Example**                                 |
| ------------ | ----------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **הֶפְסֵד**    | *hefséd*          | loss, defeat | **הֶפְסֵד כַּסְפִּי** – financial loss.     |
| **תְּבוּסָה**   | *tvusá*           | defeat, rout | **לִסְפּוֹג תְּבוּסָה** – to suffer defeat. |

## Setback, Delay, Obstacle

| **Hebrew**   | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                  | **Example**                                                |
| ------------ | ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **נְסִיגָה**   | *nesigá*          | setback, regression, retreat | **נסיגה בתהליך** – a setback in the process.               |
| **עִכּוּב**    | *ikúv*            | delay, holdup                | **עיכוב בפרויקט** – a delay in the project.                |
| **מִכְשׁוֹל**   | *michshól*        | obstacle, stumbling block    | **מכשול בדרך להצלחה** – an obstacle on the way to success. |
