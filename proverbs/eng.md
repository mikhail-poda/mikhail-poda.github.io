# Hebrew Proverbs translated to English

## Part 1

 **אין הנחתום מעיד על עיסתו**<br>
 *Ein ha-nachtom me'id al isato*<br>
 The baker does not testify about his own dough.<br>
 Self-praise is no recommendation.<br>

---

 **אין עשן בלי אש**<br>
 *Ein ashan bli esh*<br>
 There is no smoke without fire.<br>
 Where there’s smoke, there’s fire.<br>

---

 **אל תסתכל בקנקן, אלא במה שיש בו**<br>
 *Al tistakel ba-kankan, ela be-ma she-yesh bo*<br>
 Do not look at the jug, but at what is inside it.<br>
 Don’t judge a book by its cover.<br>

---

 **אל תקנה חתול בשק**<br>
 *Al tikne chatul ba-sak*<br>
 Do not buy a cat in a sack.<br>
 Don’t buy a pig in a poke.<br>

---

 **הוי זנב לאריות, ואל תהי ראש לשועלים**<br>
 *Hevei zanav la-arayot, ve-al tehi rosh la-shu'alim*<br>
 Be a tail to lions, and do not be a head to foxes.<br>
 Better to be the smallest among the great than the greatest among the small.<br>

---

 **טובה ציפור אחת ביד משתיים על העץ**<br>
 *Tova tzipor achat ba-yad mi-shtayim al ha-etz*<br>
 One bird in the hand is better than two on the tree.<br>
 A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.<br>

---

 **כי בתחבולות תעשה לך מלחמה**<br>
 *Ki ve-tachbulot ta'ase lecha milchama*<br>
 For by stratagems you shall wage war.<br>
 Use brains, not brawn.<br>
 Success in a conflict requires planning and strategy.<br>

---

 **כל המרחם על אכזרים, סופו להתאכזר על רחמנים**<br>
 *Kol ha-merachem al achzarim, sofo lehit'achzer al rachmanim*<br>
 Whoever shows mercy to the cruel will ultimately act cruelly toward the merciful.<br>
 Mercy to the guilty can be cruelty to the innocent.<br>
 Misplaced compassion toward aggressors may expose innocent people to harm.<br>

---

 **הכה בברזל בעודו חם**<br>
 *Hake ba-barzel be-odo cham*<br>
 Strike the iron while it is hot.<br>
 Strike while the iron is hot.<br>

---

 **מה ששנוא עליך, אל תעשה לחברך**<br>
 *Ma she-sanu alecha, al ta'ase le-chavercha*<br>
 What is hateful to you, do not do to your fellow.<br>
 Do not do to others what you would not want done to you.<br>

---

 **מרוב עצים לא רואים את היער**<br>
 *Merov etzim lo ro'im et ha-ya'ar*<br>
 Because of the many trees, one cannot see the forest.<br>
 You can’t see the forest for the trees.<br>

---

 **נאה דורש, נאה מקיים**<br>
 *Na'e doresh, na'e mekayem*<br>
 One who preaches well also practices well.<br>
 Practice what you preach.<br>
 Usually said approvingly of someone whose actions match their words.<br>

---

 **סנונית אחת אינה מבשרת את בוא האביב**<br>
 *Snunit achat eina mevaseret et bo ha-aviv*<br>
 One swallow does not herald the arrival of spring.<br>
 One swallow does not make a summer.<br>
 A single example is not enough to establish a general pattern.<br>

---

 **על דאטפת אטפוך, וסוף מטיפייך יטופון**<br>
 *Al de-ateft atfuch, ve-sof metifayich yetufun*<br>
 Because you drowned others, they drowned you; and in the end, those who drowned you will be drowned.<br>
 What goes around comes around.<br>
 An Aramaic saying written in Hebrew letters, expressing successive retribution.<br>

---

 **צוחק מי שצוחק אחרון**<br>
 *Tzochek mi she-tzochek acharon*<br>
 The one who laughs last is the one who laughs.<br>
 He who laughs last laughs best.<br>

---

 **צרות באות בצרורות**<br>
 *Tzarot ba'ot bitzrorot*<br>
 Troubles come in bundles.<br>
 When it rains, it pours.<br>

---

 **רחוק מהעין, רחוק מהלב**<br>
 *Rachok me-ha-ayin, rachok me-ha-lev*<br>
 Far from the eye, far from the heart.<br>
 Out of sight, out of mind.<br>

---

 **תפסת מרובה – לא תפסת**<br>
 *Tafasta merube – lo tafasta*<br>
 You grasped a great deal – you grasped nothing.<br>
 He who chases two rabbits catches neither.<br>
 Trying to accomplish too much may result in accomplishing nothing.<br>

---

 **לא כל יום פורים**<br>
 *Lo kol yom Purim*<br>
 Not every day is Purim.<br>
 Every day can’t be a holiday.<br>
 Purim is associated with celebration, feasting and merriment.<br>

---

 **כל ישראל ערבים זה בזה**<br>
 *Kol Yisrael arevim ze ba-ze*<br>
 All Israel are responsible for one another.<br>
 All for one and one for all.<br>
 **ערבים** means both “responsible for” and “guarantors for.”

---

 **ואהבת לרעך כמוך**<br>
 *Ve-ahavta le-re'acha kamocha*<br>
 You shall love your neighbor as yourself.<br>
 Love your neighbor as yourself.<br>
 A biblical commandment.<br>

---

 **איזהו עשיר? השמח בחלקו**<br>
 *Eizehu ashir? Ha-same'ach be-chelko*<br>
 Who is rich? The one who is happy with his portion.<br>
 Contentment is true wealth.<br>

---

 **איזהו חכם? הלומד מכל אדם**<br>
 *Eizehu chacham? Ha-lomed mi-kol adam*<br>
 Who is wise? The one who learns from every person.<br>
 A wise person can learn from anyone.<br>

---

 **איזהו גיבור? הכובש את יצרו**<br>
 *Eizehu gibor? Ha-kovesh et yitzro*<br>
 Who is mighty? The one who conquers his inclination.<br>
 He who conquers himself is the mightiest warrior.<br>
 **יצרו** refers to one’s impulses, urges or harmful inclinations.<br>

---

 **אם אין אני לי, מי לי?**<br>
 *Im ein ani li, mi li?*<br>
 If I am not for myself, who will be for me?<br>
 You must stand up for yourself.<br>
 This stresses personal responsibility and self-reliance.<br>

---

 **ואם לא עכשיו, אימתי?**<br>
 *Ve-im lo achshav, eimatai?*<br>
 And if not now, when?<br>
 There’s no time like the present.<br>

---

 **אמור מעט ועשה הרבה**<br>
 *Emor me'at va-ase harbe*<br>
 Say little and do much.<br>
 Actions speak louder than words.<br>


## Part 2

 **אכול ושתו כי מחר נמות**<br>
 *Achol ve-shato ki machar namut*<br>
 Eat and drink, for tomorrow we shall die.<br>
 Eat, drink, and be merry, for tomorrow we die.<br>
 A biblical expression encouraging enjoyment in the face of uncertainty, sometimes with a critical or fatalistic implication.<br>

---

 **אם אין אני לי, מי לי? וכשאני לעצמי, מה אני? ואם לא עכשיו, אימתי?**<br>
 *Im ein ani li, mi li? U-chshe-ani le-atzmi, ma ani? Ve-im lo achshav, eimatai?*<br>
 If I am not for myself, who is for me? And when I am for myself alone, what am I? And if not now, when?<br>
 God helps those who help themselves; no man is an island; there’s no time like the present.<br>

---

 **מצא מין את מינו**<br>
 *Matza min et mino*<br>
 A kind found its own kind.<br>
 Birds of a feather flock together.<br>
 Said when two people with similar personalities, interests, or faults find each other.<br>

---

 **כל אהבה שהיא תלויה בדבר, בטל דבר – בטלה אהבה; ושאינה תלויה בדבר, אינה בטלה לעולם**<br>
 *Kol ahava she-hi tluya be-davar, batel davar – batla ahava; ve-she-eina tluya be-davar, eina betela le-olam*<br>
 Any love that depends on something – when that thing ceases, the love ceases; but love that does not depend on something never ceases.<br>
 True love endures.<br>
 It distinguishes self-interested or conditional love from lasting, unconditional love.<br>

---

 **כל הפוסל במומו פוסל**<br>
 *Kol ha-posel be-mumo posel*<br>
 Whoever disqualifies another does so through his own defect.<br>
 The pot calling the kettle black.<br>
 People often condemn in others the very faults they themselves possess.<br>

---

 **עבר יומו, בטל קרבנו**<br>
 *Avar yomo, batel korbano*<br>
 Its day has passed; its sacrifice is void.<br>
 Too little, too late.<br>
 The proper time for doing something has already passed.<br>

---

 **לכל זמן, ועת לכל חפץ תחת השמים**<br>
 *La-kol zman, ve-et le-chol chefetz tachat ha-shamayim*<br>
 For everything there is a season, and a time for every purpose under heaven.<br>
 To everything there is a season.<br>

---

 **חזית איש מהיר במלאכתו? לפני מלכים יתייצב**<br>
 *Chazita ish mahir bi-melachto? Lifnei melachim yityatzev*<br>
 Have you seen a person skilled in his work? Before kings he shall stand.<br>
 A good workman is known by his work.<br>
 Exceptional ability eventually earns recognition and advancement.<br>

---

 **לעולם יאכל אדם פחות מן הראוי לו לפי ממונו, וילבש כראוי לו, ויכבד אשתו ובניו יותר מן הראוי לו**<br>
 *Le-olam yochal adam pachot min ha-ra'ui lo lefi mamono, ve-yilbash ka-ra'ui lo, vi-chabed ishto u-vanav yoter min ha-ra'ui lo*<br>
 A person should always eat less than what is appropriate to his means, dress according to his means, and honor his wife and children beyond his means.<br>
 Cut your coat according to your cloth.<br>
 Live modestly yourself, maintain a respectable appearance, and be especially generous toward your family.<br>

---

 **על ראש הגנב בוער הכובע**<br>
 *Al rosh ha-ganav bo'er ha-kova*<br>
 On the thief’s head, the hat is burning.<br>
 A guilty conscience needs no accuser.<br>
 A guilty person’s nervous behavior often exposes them.<br>

---

 **אין דבר העומד בפני הרצון**<br>
 *Ein davar ha-omed bifnei ha-ratzon*<br>
 Nothing stands in the way of the will.<br>
 Where there’s a will, there’s a way.<br>

---

 **קנה חכמה מה טוב מחרוץ, וקנות בינה נבחר מכסף**<br>
 *Kno chochma, ma tov me-charutz; u-knot bina nivchar mi-kesef*<br>
 Acquiring wisdom – how much better than gold; acquiring understanding is preferable to silver.<br>
 Wisdom is better than riches.<br>

---

 **כי ברוב חכמה רוב כעס, ויוסיף דעת יוסיף מכאוב**<br>
 *Ki ve-rov chochma rov ka'as, ve-yosif da'at yosif mach'ov*<br>
 For with much wisdom comes much vexation, and whoever increases knowledge increases pain.<br>
 Ignorance is bliss.<br>
 Greater understanding can also bring greater awareness of suffering and problems.<br>

---

 **צרת רבים חצי נחמה**<br>
 *Tzarat rabim, chatzi nechama*<br>
 The trouble of many is half a consolation.<br>
 A trouble shared is a trouble halved.<br>
 Knowing that others share the same difficulty can make it easier to bear.<br>

---

 **אילני סרק קולם הולך**<br>
 *Ilanei srak, kolam holech*<br>
 Barren trees – their sound travels far.<br>
 Empty vessels make the most noise.<br>
 People with little knowledge or substance are often the loudest.<br>

---

 **אין חכם כבעל ניסיון**<br>
 *Ein chacham ke-va'al nisayon*<br>
 There is no one as wise as a person of experience.<br>
 Experience is the best teacher.<br>

---

 **לא הביישן למד ולא הקפדן מלמד**<br>
 *Lo ha-baishan lamed, ve-lo ha-kapdan melamed*<br>
 The shy person does not learn, and the overly strict person does not teach.<br>
 He who asks a question is a fool for five minutes; he who does not ask remains a fool forever.<br>

---

 **דברי חכמים בנחת נשמעים**<br>
 *Divrei chachamim be-nachat nishma'im*<br>
 The words of the wise are heard in quiet.<br>
 A soft answer turns away wrath.<br>
 Calmly expressed wisdom is more effective than shouting.<br>

---

 **סייג לחכמה שתיקה**<br>
 *Siyag la-chochma shtika*<br>
 A fence around wisdom is silence.<br>
 Silence is golden.<br>
 Knowing when not to speak is part of wisdom.<br>

---

 **בור ששתית ממנו אל תזרוק בו אבן**<br>
 *Bor she-shatita mimenu, al tizrok bo even*<br>
 Do not throw a stone into a well from which you have drunk.<br>
 Don’t bite the hand that feeds you.<br>
 Do not harm or insult a person or institution from which you have benefited.<br>

---

 **אל יתהלל חוגר כמפתח**<br>
 *Al yithalel choger ki-mefate'ach*<br>
 Let not one who puts on his armor boast like one who takes it off.<br>
 Don’t count your chickens before they hatch.<br>
 Do not boast of victory before the struggle has even begun.<br>

---

 **חושך שבטו שונא בנו**<br>
 *Chosech shivto, sone beno*<br>
 He who spares his rod hates his son.<br>
 Spare the rod and spoil the child.<br>
 An ancient statement advocating firm discipline; it should not automatically be read as modern approval of corporal punishment.<br>

---

 **שלח לחמך על פני המים, כי ברוב הימים תמצאנו**<br>
 *Shalach lachmecha al pnei ha-mayim, ki ve-rov ha-yamim timtza'enu*<br>
 Cast your bread upon the waters, for after many days you will find it.<br>
 Cast your bread upon the waters.<br>
 Generosity or a worthwhile investment may eventually bring an unexpected return.<br>

---

 **טובים השניים מן האחד**<br>
 *Tovim ha-shnayim min ha-echad*<br>
 Two are better than one.<br>
 Two heads are better than one.<br>

## Part 3

 **סייג לחכמה שתיקה**<br>
 *Siyag la-chochma shtika*<br>
 A fence around wisdom is silence.<br>
 Silence is golden.<br>
 Knowing when not to speak is part of wisdom.<br>

---

 **אמור לי מי הם חבריך ואומר לך מי אתה**<br>
 *Emor li mi hem chaverecha, ve-omar lecha mi ata*<br>
 Tell me who your friends are, and I will tell you who you are.<br>
 A man is known by the company he keeps.<br>

---

 **מי שהולך לישון עם כלבים, שלא יתפלא אם הוא קם עם פרעושים**<br>
 *Mi she-holech lishon im klavim, she-lo yitpale im hu kam im par'oshim*<br>
 Whoever goes to sleep with dogs should not be surprised if he wakes up with fleas.<br>
 Lie down with dogs, wake up with fleas.<br>
 The people one associates with influence one’s conduct and reputation.<br>

---

 **אל תדון את חברך עד שתגיע למקומו**<br>
 *Al tadun et chavercha ad she-tagia li-mkomo*<br>
 Do not judge your fellow until you have reached his place.<br>
 Don’t judge a man until you have walked a mile in his shoes.<br>
 The source also gives the older form **אל תדין**.<br>

---

 **סוף גנב לתלייה**<br>
 *Sof ganav li-tliya*<br>
 A thief’s end is hanging.<br>
 Crime doesn’t pay.<br>
 Wrongdoing will eventually bring punishment.<br>

---

 **יצא מרצע מן השק**<br>
 *Yatza martze'a min ha-sak*<br>
 The awl came out of the sack.<br>
 The cat is out of the bag.<br>
 Something concealed has been exposed.<br>

---

 **נתנו לחתול לשמור על השמנת**<br>
 *Natnu la-chatul lishmor al ha-shamenet*<br>
 They gave the cat the cream to guard.<br>
 Set a fox to guard the henhouse.<br>
 Someone is entrusted with protecting the very thing they are likely to take.<br>

---

 **אל יתהלל חוגר כמפתח**<br>
 *Al yithalel choger ki-mefate'ach*<br>
 Let one who puts on his armor not boast like one who takes it off.<br>
 Don’t count your chickens before they hatch.<br>
 Do not boast of success before the undertaking is finished.<br>

---

 **אין חכם כבעל ניסיון**<br>
 *Ein chacham ke-va'al nisayon*<br>
 No one is as wise as a person with experience.<br>
 Experience is the best teacher.<br>

---

 **לא בוכים על חלב שנשפך**<br>
 *Lo bochim al chalav she-nishpach*<br>
 One does not cry over milk that has been spilled.<br>
 Don’t cry over spilt milk.<br>

---

 **אין עשן בלי אש**<br>
 *Ein ashan bli esh*<br>
 There is no smoke without fire.<br>
 Where there’s smoke, there’s fire.<br>

---

 **עברנו את פרעה, נעבור גם את זה**<br>
 *Avarnu et Par'o, na'avor gam et ze*<br>
 We survived Pharaoh; we will survive this too.<br>
 This too shall pass.<br>
 A humorous reassurance that the present difficulty can also be overcome.<br>

---

 **הדג מסריח מהראש**<br>
 *Ha-dag masri'ach me-ha-rosh*<br>
 The fish stinks from the head.<br>
 A fish rots from the head down.<br>
 Problems in an organization commonly begin with its leadership.<br>

---

 **ההר הוליד עכבר**<br>
 *Ha-har holid achbar*<br>
 The mountain gave birth to a mouse.<br>
 Much ado about nothing.<br>
 Great expectations or commotion produce a very small result.<br>

---

 **החיים הם לא פיקניק**<br>
 *Ha-chayim hem lo piknik*<br>
 Life is not a picnic.<br>
 Life isn’t a bed of roses.<br>

---

 **מצאו ש״ג**<br>
 *Matz'u shin-gimel*<br>
 They found a gate guard.<br>

 **ש״ג** is an abbreviation for a low-ranking military gate guard. The expression means that a minor employee has been made the scapegoat.<br>

---

 **הדשא של השכן ירוק יותר**<br>
 *Ha-deshe shel ha-shachen yarok yoter*<br>
 The neighbor’s grass is greener.<br>
 The grass is always greener on the other side.<br>

---

 **אצבע קלה על ההדק**<br>
 *Etzba kala al ha-hedek*<br>
 A light finger on the trigger.<br>
 To be trigger-happy.<br>
 Said of someone who resorts to force or drastic action too readily.<br>

---

 **הלילה עוד צעיר**<br>
 *Ha-layla od tza'ir*<br>
 The night is still young.<br>
 The night is young.<br>

---

 **חבר נבחן בעת צרה**<br>
 *Chaver nivchan be-et tzara*<br>
 A friend is tested in a time of trouble.<br>
 A friend in need is a friend indeed.<br>

---

 **כבדהו וחשדהו**<br>
 *Kabdehu ve-chashdehu*<br>
 Respect him and suspect him.<br>
 Trust, but verify.<br>
 Treat others respectfully without abandoning reasonable caution.<br>

---

 **כל התחלות קשות**<br>
 *Kol ha-hatchalot kashot*<br>
 All beginnings are difficult.<br>
 The first step is the hardest.<br>

---

 **כלב נובח לא נושך**<br>
 *Kelev nove'ach lo noshech*<br>
 A barking dog does not bite.<br>
 Barking dogs seldom bite.<br>

---

 **כלב נובח ושיירה עוברת**<br>
 *Kelev nove'ach ve-shayara overet*<br>
 The dog barks and the caravan passes.<br>
 The dogs bark, but the caravan moves on.<br>
 Criticism and noise need not stop continued progress.<br>

---

 **מטאטא חדש מטאטא טוב**<br>
 *Matate chadash metate tov*<br>
 A new broom sweeps well.<br>
 A new broom sweeps clean.<br>

---

 **נכנס יין, יצא סוד**<br>
 *Nichnas yayin, yatza sod*<br>
 Wine entered; a secret came out.<br>
 In vino veritas.<br>
 Alcohol loosens the tongue and causes people to reveal what they normally conceal.<br>

---

 **סוף מעשה במחשבה תחילה**<br>
 *Sof ma'ase be-machshava techila*<br>
 The end of an action lies in thought at the beginning.<br>
 Look before you leap.<br>
 Think through the desired result before acting.<br>

---

 **עד החתונה יעבור**<br>
 *Ad ha-chatuna ya'avor*<br>
 It will pass by the wedding.<br>

 A humorous reassurance, especially to a child, that a minor injury or problem will soon heal.<br>

---

 **על טעם וריח אין להתווכח**<br>
 *Al ta'am va-re'ach ein lehitvake'ach*<br>
 There is no arguing about taste and smell.<br>
 There’s no accounting for taste.<br>

---	

 **עם האוכל בא התיאבון**<br>
 *Im ha-ochel ba ha-te'avon*<br>
 Appetite comes with eating.<br>
 Appetite comes with eating.<br>
 Beginning an activity often increases one’s desire for more.<br>

---

 **צוחק מי שצוחק אחרון**<br>
 *Tzochek mi she-tzochek acharon*<br>
 The one who laughs last is the one who laughs.<br>
 He who laughs last laughs best.<br>

---

 **על ראש הגנב בוער הכובע**<br>
 *Al rosh ha-ganav bo'er ha-kova*<br>
 The hat is burning on the thief’s head.<br>
 A guilty conscience needs no accuser.<br>
 A guilty person often exposes themselves through nervous behavior.<br>

---

 **טוחנים מים**<br>
 *Tochanim mayim*<br>
 They are grinding water.<br>

 To engage repeatedly in pointless discussion or activity without producing anything.<br>

---

 **פרצה קוראת לגנב**<br>
 *Pirtza koret la-ganav*<br>
 A breach calls to the thief.<br>
 Opportunity makes the thief.<br>
 Poor security or easy opportunity can tempt people into wrongdoing.<br>

---

 **לא כל הנוצץ זהב הוא**<br>
 *Lo kol ha-notzetz zahav hu*<br>
 Not everything that glitters is gold.<br>
 All that glitters is not gold.<br>
 The source also contains the variant **לא כל דבר שנוצץ הוא זהב**.<br>

---

 **התפוח לא נופל רחוק מהעץ**<br>
 *Ha-tapu'ach lo nofel rachok me-ha-etz*<br>
 The apple does not fall far from the tree.<br>
 The apple doesn’t fall far from the tree.<br>

---

 **נחיה ונראה**<br>
 *Nichye ve-nir'e*<br>
 We shall live and see.<br>
 Time will tell.<br>

---

 **מכל מלמדיי השכלתי**<br>
 *Mi-kol melamdai hiskalti*<br>
 From all my teachers I gained understanding.<br>
 You can learn something from everyone.<br>
 Originally a biblical expression.<br>

---

 **תפסת מרובה – לא תפסת**<br>
 *Tafasta merube – lo tafasta*<br>
 You grasped a great deal –you grasped nothing.<br>
 Don’t bite off more than you can chew.<br>

 **אין חדש תחת השמש**<br>

 **דרך ארץ קדמה לתורה**<br>

 Proper conduct preceded the Torah.<br>

 *Halach ha-zarzir etzel ha-orev*<br>
 The starling went to the raven.<br>
 Birds of a feather flock together.<br>
 Usually said negatively about two people who suit one another because they share the same faults.<br>

---

 **הסנדלר הולך יחף**<br>
 *Ha-sandlar holech yachef*<br>
 The shoemaker goes barefoot.<br>

 A professional may neglect the same need in their own life that they address for others.<br>

 **מן הפח אל הפחת**<br>

 Out of the frying pan and into the fire.<br>

 One does not rely on a miracle.<br>
 God helps those who help themselves.<br>

 **רצונו של אדם – כבודו**<br>

 A person’s wish is his honor.<br>

 **הזאב שבע והכבשה שלמה**<br>
 *Ha-ze'ev save'a ve-ha-kivsa shlema*<br>
 The wolf is satisfied and the sheep remains whole.<br>

 A compromise or solution that satisfies one side without harming the other.<br>

---

 **להוסיף שמן למדורה**<br>

 To add oil to the bonfire.<br>
 Add fuel to the fire.<br>

 As you sow, so shall you reap.<br>
 Preparation and work must precede enjoyment of their results.<br>

---

 **מרוב עצים לא רואים את היער**<br>
 *Me-rov etzim lo ro'im et ha-ya'ar*<br>
 Because of the many trees, one cannot see the forest.<br>
 You can’t see the forest for the trees.<br>

---

 **אין אדם נתפס בשעת צערו**<br>
 *Ein adam nitpas bi-sh'at tza'aro*<br>
 A person is not held accountable in a moment of distress.<br>

 Words or actions spoken in intense grief should not be judged by ordinary standards.<br>

---

 **אינו יודע בין ימינו לשמאלו**<br>
 *Eino yode'a bein yemino li-smolo*<br>
 He does not know between his right and his left.<br>
 He doesn’t know his right hand from his left.<br>

---

 **כל הפוסל במומו פוסל**<br>
 *Kol ha-posel be-mumo posel*<br>
 Whoever disqualifies another does so through his own defect.<br>
 The pot calling the kettle black.<br>
 People frequently condemn in others the faults they themselves possess.<br>

---

 **לא הכובע קובע ולא המעיל מועיל**<br>
 *Lo ha-kova kove'a ve-lo ha-me'il mo'il*<br>
 It is not the hat that determines, nor the coat that improves.<br>
 Clothes do not make the man.<br>
 The Hebrew is a rhyming expression.<br>

---

 **להכניס ראש בריא למיטה חולה**<br>
 *Lehachnis rosh bari le-mita chola*<br>
 To put a healthy head into a sickbed.<br>
 Don’t borrow trouble.<br>
 To involve oneself unnecessarily in a problematic or dangerous situation.<br>

---

 **חכם אחרי מעשה**<br>
 *Chacham acharei ma'ase*<br>
 Wise after the event.<br>
 Hindsight is twenty-twenty.<br>
 Said critically of someone who knows what should have been done only after the outcome is known.<br>

 *Al tihye tzodek – tihye chacham*<br>
 Do not be right – be wise.<br>

 Character is revealed through financial behavior, conduct while drinking, and behavior when angry.<br>

---

 *Tzipiyot yesh rak ba-kariyot*<br>
 There are expectations only on pillows.<br>

 **שאיפות יש רק בג׳וינט**<br>

 Aspirations exist only in a joint.<br>

 A slang pun: **שאיפות** means both “aspirations” and “inhalations” or “puffs.”

---

 **אל תשווה בין מחט לתחת**<br>
 *Al tashve bein machat la-tachat*<br>
 Do not compare a needle with a backside.<br>
 Don’t compare apples and oranges.<br>
 A deliberately crude rhyme used when two things are not reasonably comparable.<br>
