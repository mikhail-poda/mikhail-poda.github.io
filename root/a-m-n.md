# Root: א־מ־ן

## Meaning: Belief, Trust, Reliability

| **Hebrew**  | **Transcription** | **Meaning**              | **Example**                                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **לְהַאֲמִין** | *leha'amin*       | to believe               | **אני מאמין לך.** (*Ani ma'amin lecha.*) – I believe you.                                                                             |
| **אֱמוּנָה**  | *emuna*           | belief, faith            | **הוא איבד את האמונה שלו.** (*Hu ibed et ha-emuna shelo.*) – He lost his faith.                                                       |
| **אֵמוּן**   | *emun*            | trust                    | **יש ביניהם אמון מלא.** (*Yesh beineihem emun male.*) – There is complete trust between them.                                         |
| **נֶאֱמָן**   | *ne'eman*         | loyal, faithful          | **הוא נשאר נאמן לחברים שלו.** (*Hu nish'ar ne'eman la-chaverim shelo.*) – He remained loyal to his friends.                           |
| **אָמִין**   | *amin*            | reliable, credible       | **זה מקור מידע אמין.** (*Ze makor meida amin.*) – This is a reliable source of information.                                           |
| **אֲמִינוּת** | *aminut*          | reliability, credibility | **חשוב לבדוק את אמינות המידע.** (*Chashuv livdok et aminut ha-meida.*) – It is important to check the reliability of the information. |

### Note 1

* **אֱמוּנָה** (*emuna*) – belief or faith
* **אֵמוּן** (*emun*) – trust
* **אָמִין** (*amin*) – reliable or credible
* **נֶאֱמָן** (*ne'eman*) – faithful or loyal

## Meaning: Training, Practice

| **Hebrew**   | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                     | **Example**                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ----------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **לְאַמֵּן**    | *le'amen*         | to train, coach                 | **הוא מאמן את קבוצת הכדורגל.** (*Hu me'amen et kvutzat ha-kaduregel.*) – He coaches the football team.              |
| **לְהִתְאַמֵּן**  | *lehit'amen*      | to train, practice, work out    | **אני מתאמן שלוש פעמים בשבוע.** (*Ani mit'amen shalosh pe'amim ba-shavua.*) – I work out three times a week.        |
| **אִימּוּן**   | *imun*            | training, practice, workout     | **האימון מתחיל בשש בערב.** (*Ha-imun matchil be-shesh ba-erev.*) – The training starts at six in the evening.       |
| **מְאַמֵּן**    | *me'amen*         | coach, trainer                  | **המאמן נתן לשחקנים הוראות.** (*Ha-me'amen natan la-sachkanim hora'ot.*) – The coach gave the players instructions. |
| **מִתְאַמֵּן**   | *mit'amen*        | trainee; person who is training | **כל מתאמן קיבל תוכנית אישית.** (*Kol mit'amen kibel tochnit ishit.*) – Each trainee received a personal program.   |

### Note 2

A particularly useful distinction:

* **אֵמוּן** (*emun*) – trust
* **אִימּוּן** (*imun*) – training

Compare:

**יש לי אמון בו.** (*Yesh li emun bo.*) – I trust him.<br>
**יש לי אימון היום.** (*Yesh li imun ha-yom.*) – I have training today.

## Meaning: Art and Craftsmanship

| **Hebrew**    | **Transcription** | **Meaning**             | **Example**                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **אָמָּן**      | *oman*            | artist                  | **הוא אמן ישראלי מוכר.** (*Hu oman Israeli mukar.*) – He is a well-known Israeli artist.                                               |
| **אָמָּנוּת**    | *omanut*          | art                     | **היא לומדת אמנות באוניברסיטה.** (*Hi lomedet omanut ba-universita.*) – She studies art at university.                                 |
| **אָמָּנוּתִי**   | *omanuti*         | artistic                | **יש לסרט ערך אמנותי רב.** (*Yesh la-seret erech omanuti rav.*) – The film has great artistic value.                                   |
| **אוּמָּן**     | *uman*            | craftsman, artisan      | **האומן יצר את הכלי בעבודת יד.** (*Ha-uman yatzar et ha-kli ba-avodat yad.*) – The craftsman made the object by hand.                  |
| **אוּמָּנוּת**   | *umanut*          | craft, craftsmanship    | **העבודה הזאת דורשת אומנות רבה.** (*Ha-avoda ha-zot doreshet umanut raba.*) – This work requires great craftsmanship.                  |
| **רַב־אָמָּן**   | *rav-oman*        | master artist, virtuoso | **הוא נחשב לרב־אמן בתחום המוזיקה.** (*Hu nechshav le-rav-oman bi-tchum ha-muzika.*) – He is considered a master in the field of music. |

### Note 3

There are two closely related but distinct words:

* **אָמָּן** (*oman*) – artist
* **אוּמָּן** (*uman*) – craftsman, artisan

## The Root at a Glance

The root **א־מ־ן** has several meanings that seem surprisingly distant from one another:

| **Idea**                 | **Examples**                  |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| belief and trust         | **להאמין, אמונה, אמון, נאמן** |
| training and practice    | **לאמן, להתאמן, אימון, מאמן** |
| art and craftsmanship    | **אמן, אמנות, אומן, אומנות**  |
| agreement or affirmation | **אמן, אמנה**                 |
