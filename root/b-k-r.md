# Root: ב־ק־ר

## Meaning: Morning

| **Hebrew** | **Transcription** | **Meaning** | **Example**                                                                             |
| ---------- | ----------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **בּוֹקֶר**   | *boker*           | morning     | **אני שותה קפה כל בוקר.** (*Ani shote kafe kol boker.*) – I drink coffee every morning. |

## Meaning: Criticism, Inspection, Control

| **Hebrew**    | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                      | **Example**                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **לְבַקֵּר**     | *levaker*         | to criticize; inspect            | **העיתון ביקר את החלטת הממשלה.** (*Ha-iton biker et hachlatat ha-memshala.*) – The newspaper criticized the government's decision.     |
| **בִּקֹּרֶת**     | *bikoret*         | criticism, review, inspection    | **הסרט קיבל ביקורות טובות.** (*Ha-seret kibel bikorot tovot.*) – The film received good reviews.                                       |
| **מְבַקֵּר**     | *mevaker*         | critic, inspector, auditor       | **המבקר מצא כמה בעיות בדוח.** (*Ha-mevaker matza kama be'ayot ba-do'ach.*) – The auditor found several problems in the report.         |
| **בַּקָּרָה**     | *bakara*          | control, supervision, monitoring | **המערכת משמשת לבקרה על התהליך.** (*Ha-ma'arechet meshameshet le-bakara al ha-tahalich.*) – The system is used to monitor the process. |

### Note 1

**לְבַקֵּר** (*levaker*) has two very common meanings:

* to visit
* to criticize

Compare:

**ביקרתי את החבר שלי.** (*Bikarti et ha-chaver sheli.*) – I visited my friend.<br>
**ביקרתי את ההתנהגות שלו.** (*Bikarti et ha-hitnahagut shelo.*) – I criticized his behavior.

The intended meaning normally becomes obvious from context.

### Note 2

**מְבַקֵּר** (*mevaker*) has an unusually wide range of meanings:

* visitor
* critic
* inspector
* auditor

For example:

**מבקר במסעדה** can be a visitor/customer, while **מבקר מסעדות** is a restaurant critic.

## בִּיקּוֹרֶת vs. בַּקָּרָה

* **בִּיקּוֹרֶת** (*bikoret*) – criticism, review, inspection; someone examines or evaluates something.
* **בַּקָּרָה** (*bakara*) – control, supervision, monitoring; a process or system is kept under observation or control.

Examples:

**ביקורת איכות** (*bikoret eichut*) – quality inspection<br>
**בקרת איכות** (*bakarat eichut*) – quality control<br>
**ביקורת ספרות** (*bikoret sifrut*) – literary criticism<br>
**מערכת בקרה** (*ma'arechet bakara*) – control system

## Other

| **Hebrew** | **Transcription** | **Meaning** | **Example**                                                                    |
| ---------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **בָּקָר**    | *bakar*           | cattle      | **החקלאי מגדל בקר.** (*Ha-chaklai megadel bakar.*) – The farmer raises cattle. |

### Note 3

The three unpointed letters **בקר** can therefore hide several very different words:

* **בּוֹקֶר** (*boker*) – morning
* **בָּקָר** (*bakar*) – cattle
* **בִּיקֵּר** (*biker*) – he visited / criticized
* **בַּקָּר** (*bakar*) – controller, inspector
