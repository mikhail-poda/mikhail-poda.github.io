# Topic: to See, Watch

| **Infinitive**  | **Transcription**   | **Meaning**                     | **Notes**                                     | **Example**                                        |
|-----------------|---------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **לִרְאוֹת**     | *lir'ot*             | to see                          | Most basic verb for seeing                      |  **אני רואה אותך** – I see you                   |
| **לְהִסְתַּכֵּל**    | *lehistakel*         | to look at, watch               | Active looking, requires **"על"**               | **להסתכל על התמונה** – look at the picture       |
| **לִצְפּוֹת**     | *litzpot*            | to watch, observe               | Formal or for media, requires **"ב־"**          |  **לצפות בסרט** – watch a movie                   |
| **לְהַשְׁקִיף**    | *lehashkif*          | to overlook, view from above    | Formal or scenic                                |  **השקפנו מהגג** – we looked out from the roof    |
| **לְהִתְבּוֹנֵן**   | *lehitbonen*         | to observe carefully            | Deep, thoughtful observation; often used for mindfulness or reflection | **הוא התבונן בציור במשך שעה** – he observed the painting for an hour  |
| **לְהַבִּיט**     | *lehabit*            | to gaze, look                   |   Slightly formal, requires **"ב־"**            | **להביט בילד** – look at the child                |
| **לִבְהוֹת**     | *livhot*             | to stare blankly                | Rare; implies spaced-out or frozen look         |  **בהה בקיר** – stared at the wall                 |

## The Letter צ and the Eyes

Interestingly, **צ** appears in several verbs connected with watching or movements of the eyes. 
This is a noticeable vocabulary cluster, although the verbs belong to different roots and do not form one historical word family.

| **Infinitive**   | **Transcription** | **Meaning**        | **Example**                                                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **לִצְפּוֹת**       | *litzpot*       | to watch, observe     | **לצפות בסרט** – watch a movie                                         |
| **לְהָצִיץ**       | *lehatzitz*     | to peek, glance       | **הוא הציץ דרך הדלת** – he peeked through the door                     |
| **לַעֲצוֹם**       | *la’atzom*      | to close one’s eyes   | **עצמתי את העיניים** – I closed my eyes<br>**עיניים עצומות לרווחה** – Eyes Wide Shut (movie) |
| **לְמַצְמֵץ**       | *lematzmetz*    | to blink              | **היא מצמצה בגלל האור החזק** – she blinked because of the bright light |
| **לִקְרוֹץ**       | *likrotz*       | to wink               | **היא קרצה לי** – she winked at me                                     |
