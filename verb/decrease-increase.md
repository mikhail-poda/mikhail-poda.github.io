# Topic: to Decrease and to Increase

## to Decrease, Reduce, Lower, Shrink

| **Hebrew**     | **Transcription** | **Meaning**                                                                            | **Example**                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **לְהוֹרִיד**    | *lehorid*         | to lower, reduce, bring down                                                           | **הם הורידו את המחיר.** (*hem horidu et ha-mechir*) – They lowered the price.                                                             |
| **לָרֶדֶת**      | *laredet*         | to go down, decline, or decrease in level or value                                     | **הטמפרטורה ירדה בלילה.** (*ha-temperatura yarda ba-laila*) – The temperature went down during the night.                                 |
| **לִדְעוֹךְ**     | *lid'och*         | to fade, to wane                                                                       | **ההתלהבות דעכה עם הזמן.** (*ha-hitlahavut da'acha im ha-zman*) – The enthusiasm faded over time.                                         |
| **לְהַחְסִיר**    | *lehachsir*       | to subtract, deduct; to omit or leave out                                              | **המורה החסיר חמש נקודות מן הציון.** (*ha-more hechsir chamesh nekudot min ha-tziyun*) – The teacher deducted five points from the grade. |
| **לְחַסֵּר**      | *lechaser*        | to subtract or deduct, especially in arithmetic                                        | **צריך לחסר שלוש מעשר.** (*tzarich lechaser shalosh me-eser*) – You need to subtract three from ten.                                      |
| **לְהַמְעִיט**    | *leham'it*        | to reduce the amount or frequency of something; to do or use less, usually with **ב־** | **כדאי להמעיט בצריכת סוכר.** (*kedai leham'it bitzrichat sukar*) – It is advisable to reduce sugar consumption.                           |
| **לְהַנְמִיךְ**    | *lehanmich*       | to lower in height, volume, pitch, or tone                                             | **הוא הנמיך את הקול.** (*hu hinmich et ha-kol*) – He lowered his voice.                                                                   |
| **לִפְחוֹת**     | *lifchot*         | to decrease, to become less; general intransitive decrease                             | **מספר התאונות פחת השנה.** (*mispar ha-te'unot pachat ha-shana*) – The number of accidents decreased this year.                |
| **לְהַפְחִית**    | *lehafchit*       | to reduce, lessen, decrease something                                                  | **החברה הפחיתה את ההוצאות.** (*ha-chevra hifchita et ha-hotza'ot*) – The company reduced its expenses.                                    |
| **לְהִצְטַמְצֵם**   | *lehitztamtzem*   | to shrink, become smaller or more limited in scope                                     | **היקף הפעילות הצטמצם.** (*hekef ha-pe'ilut hitztamtzem*) – The scope of activity shrank.                                                 |
| **לְצַמְצֵם**     | *letzamtzem*      | to reduce, cut back, restrict, or narrow the scope of something                        | **החברה צמצמה את כוח האדם.** (*ha-chevra tzimtzema et ko'ach ha-adam*) – The company reduced its workforce.                               |
| **לִקְטוֹן**     | *likton*          | to diminish or become smaller; mostly formal or literary                               | **הסיכוי יקטן אם נחכה.** (*ha-sikui yiktan im nechake*) – The chance will diminish if we wait.                                            |
| **לְהַקְטִין**    | *lehaktin*        | to make smaller; to reduce in size or magnitude                                        | **הם הקטינו את התמונה.** (*hem hiktinu et ha-tmuna*) – They made the picture smaller.                                                     |
| **לְהָקֵל**      | *lehakel*         | to ease, alleviate, or make something less severe or burdensome                        | **התרופה הקלה על הכאב.** (*ha-trufa hekela al ha-ke'ev*) – The medicine alleviated the pain.                                              |
| **לְמַזְעֵר**     | *lemaz'er*        | to minimize; to reduce to the smallest possible extent                                 | **המערכת נועדה למזער את הסיכון.** (*ha-ma'arechet no'ada lemaz'er et ha-sikun*) – The system is designed to minimize the risk.            |
| **לְקַצֵּר**      | *lekatzer*        | to shorten in length or duration                                                       | **הם קיצרו את הפגישה.** (*hem kitzru et ha-pgisha*) – They shortened the meeting.                                                         |
| **לְהִתְקַצֵּר**    | *lehitkatzer*     | to become shorter in length or duration                                                | **הימים מתקצרים בחורף.** (*ha-yamim mitkatzrim ba-choref*) – The days become shorter in winter.                                           |
| **לִפּוֹל**      | *lipol*           | to fall or drop, often considerably or suddenly                                        | **המכירות נפלו בעשרים אחוזים.** (*ha-mchirot naflu b'esrim achuzim*) – Sales fell by twenty percent.                                      |
| **לְהִתְכַּוֵּץ**    | *lehitkavetz*     | to shrink or contract, physically or economically                                      | **הבד התכווץ בכביסה.** (*ha-bad hitkavetz ba-kvisa*) – The fabric shrank in the wash.                                                     |

### Summary

| **Subcategory**                         | **Relevant Verbs**                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **General decrease / become smaller**   | לִפְחוֹת, לִקְטוֹן                                  |
| **To lower / go down / fall**              | לְהוֹרִיד, לָרֶדֶת, לִפּוֹל, לְהַנְמִיךְ                |
| **To Reduce / limit / minimize**           | לְהַפְחִית, לְצַמְצֵם, לְהַקְטִין, לְמַזְעֵר, לְהַמְעִיט     |
| **To shrink / contract**                   | לְהִצְטַמְצֵם, לְהִתְכַּוֵּץ                            |
| **To shorten**                             | לְקַצֵּר, לְהִתְקַצֵּר                               |
| **To fade / weaken / become less intense** | לִדְעוֹךְ, לְהָקֵל                                |
| **To subtract / deduct / leave out**       | לְחַסֵּר, לְהַחְסִיר                               |

## to Increase, Expand, Raise, Multiply

| **Hebrew**    | **Transcription**  | **Meaning**             | **Example** |  
|---------------|--------------------|-------------------------|-------------|  
| **לִגְדּוֹל**     | *ligdol*          | to grow, to increase in size | **העץ גדל במהירות.** (*ha-etz gadal b'mehirut*) – The tree grew quickly. |  
| **לְהַגְדִּיל**    | *lehagdil*        | to enlarge, to make bigger | **הם הגדילו את התקציב.** (*hem hegdelu et ha-takziv*) – They increased the budget. |  
| **לִצְמוֹחַ**     | *litzmoach*       | to sprout, to grow naturally | **העסק צמח במהירות.** (*ha-esek tzamach b'mehirut*) – The business grew rapidly. |  
| **לְהִתְרַבּוֹת**   | *lehitrabot*      | to multiply, to become more numerous | **השאלות התרבו עם הזמן.** (*ha-she'elot hitrabu im ha-zman*) – The questions multiplied over time. |  
| **לְהַגְבִּיר**    | *lehagbir*        | to amplify, to intensify | **צריך להגביר את הווליום.** (*tzarich lehagbir et ha-volume*) – You need to increase the volume. |  
| **לַעֲלוֹת**     | *la'alot*         | to rise, go up, or increase in level, amount, or value | **המחירים עלו בשנה האחרונה.** (*ha-mechirim alu ba-shana ha-achrona*) – Prices increased over the past year.           |
| **לְהַעֲלוֹת**    | *leha'alot*       | to raise, to bring up | **הם העלו את המחירים.** (*hem he'elu et ha-mechirim*) – They raised the prices. |  
| **לְהָרִים**     | *leharim*         | to lift, to raise | **הוא הרים את השק הכבד.** (*hu herim et ha-sak ha-kaved*) – He lifted the heavy bag. |  
| **לְהַרְחִיב**    | *leharhiv*        | to expand, to widen | **הם הרחיבו את הכביש.** (*hem hirchivu et ha-kvish*) – They expanded the road. |  
| **לְהִתְרַחֵב**    | *lehitrachav*     | to expand, become wider, or grow in scope              | **החברה התרחבה לשווקים חדשים.** (*ha-chevra hitracheva le-shvakim chadashim*) – The company expanded into new markets. |
| **לְהַאֲרִיךְ**    | *leha'arich*      | to extend, to prolong | **הם האריכו את השיחה.** (*hem hearichu et ha-sicha*) – They extended the conversation. |  
| **לְהַגְבִּיהַּ**    | *lehagbia*        | to raise, to elevate | **הם הגביהו את החומה.** (*hem higbi'u et ha-choma*) – They raised the wall. |  
| **לְהַכְפִּיל**    | *lehachpil*       | to double | **החברה הכפילה את הכנסותיה.** (*ha-chevra hichpila et hachnasoteha*) – The company doubled its revenue. |  
| **לְשַׁלֵּשׁ**      | *leshalesh*       | to triple | **המכירות שולשו בשנה האחרונה.** (*ha-mkirot shulshu ba-shana ha-achrona*) – The sales tripled in the last year. |

### Summary

| **To rise / go up**                    | לַעֲלוֹת                       |

| **To expand / widen**                  | לְהִתְרַחֵב, לְהַרְחִיב              |
| **To extend / prolong**                | לְהַאֲרִיךְ                       |
| **To raise / elevate**                 | לְהַעֲלוֹת, לְהָרִים, לְהַגְבִּיהַּ       |
| **To intensify / amplify**             | לְהַגְבִּיר                       |
